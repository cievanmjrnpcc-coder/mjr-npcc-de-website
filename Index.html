Team Structure

Supported by a clear editorial structure.

Every member contributes differently, but all roles work toward the same standard of clear, reliable and professional documentation.

CE OVERALL LEADERSHIP

Chief Editor

Leads the team, sets priorities, approves major work and maintains overall editorial standards.

DCE DEPUTY LEADERSHIP

Deputy Chief Editor

Coordinates priorities, monitors performance and supports continuity across editorial work.

ACE TEAM COORDINATION

Assistant Chief Editor

Supports training, task coordination, quality assurance and junior member development.

SDE SENIOR EDITORIAL RESPONSIBILITY

Senior Editor

Handles complex work, reviews quality and provides guidance across specialist editorial appointments.

DE DOCUMENTATION APPOINTMENT

Document Editor

Creates, reviews and maintains documents, guides and structured resources.

DBE DATABASE APPOINTMENT

Database Editor

This role is currently unavailable and will be reintroduced in the future. Maintains records, data ownership, organisation and approved access.

CME COMMUNICATION APPOINTMENT

Communication Editor

Supports notices, announcements and presentation of information.

DA TRAINING APPOINTMENT

Assistant Editor

Learns foundational skills and completes guided editorial practice before progressing.

SC PROSPECTIVE MEMBER

Selected Candidate

Receives an invitation to learn about the team before deciding whether to begin training.

◎
Guidance is available throughout the journey

New members are not expected to work alone. Senior editors and team leaders provide feedback, examples and support as members develop.

Development Pathway

A clear journey from learner to leader.

Progression is based on training, reliability, contribution, teamwork and the ability to maintain editorial standards.

01
ENTRY AND ORIENTATION

Assistant Editor

Learns the team's purpose, basic standards, Google Workspace tools, formatting principles and safe handling of internal information.

Complete foundational modules Practise core editing tools Understand review procedures
02
GUIDED CONTRIBUTION

Document / Communication / Database Editor

Develops within a specialist editorial pathway while applying established formatting, communication, information management and review standards.

Complete specialist editorial work Respond to review feedback Maintain consistent standards
03
INDEPENDENT RESPONSIBILITY

Senior Document Editor

Handles more complex work, supports junior members and helps maintain quality across assigned editorial areas.

Lead selected document updates Review junior members' work Support training and guidance
04
TEAM COORDINATION

Assistant Chief Editor

Coordinates assigned workstreams, supports training delivery and assists with quality assurance and team administration.

Coordinate task completion Monitor document standards Support member development
05
OPERATIONAL LEADERSHIP

Deputy Chief Editor

Oversees editorial operations, coordinates team priorities and supports the Chief Editor in maintaining continuity and standards.

Coordinate major editorial projects Review progress and workload Support leadership decisions
06
OVERALL DIRECTION

Chief Editor

Leads the team, sets priorities, maintains editorial standards and ensures the Editorial Team continues to support the unit effectively.

Set team direction and standards Approve major editorial work Develop future leaders
◎

Titles reflect responsibility, not seniority alone. Promotion depends on consistent performance, conduct, reliability and readiness to support others.

What We Look For

You do not need to be perfect on day one.

What matters is whether you are willing to learn, improve and contribute responsibly.

01

Reliability

Complete work carefully and follow through on assigned responsibilities.

02
