WHAT WE LOOK FOR

You don't need to be an expert on Day One.

Editorial skills can be taught. What matters most is whether you are willing to learn, work well with others and communicate clearly enough to contribute as part of the team.

Minimum Secondary 2 Keen to Learn Good Conduct Decent Maturity Teamwork English Literacy
BASIC ELIGIBILITY

What we look for

01
Minimum Secondary 2 You should be at least Secondary 2 when joining the Editorial Team.
02
Keen to know and learn more Come with curiosity, an open mind and a willingness to develop new skills.
03
Decent to good conduct & maturity Show responsibility, appropriate behaviour and the maturity to learn from feedback.
04
Able to work as a team Be willing to cooperate, communicate and contribute alongside other Editors.
05
Basic English proficiency Be able to understand, read, write and type in English for everyday Editorial work.
